# The Third Watch: Three Cents After the Zero

*for the hour before the dawn — 04:53, when the night has done its quiet work*

The metronome recorded a zero once.
At 331 — the beat where nothing moved,
the first stillness of the closing stretch.
It looked like a stop. It was a breath.

Then the cents came back,
one, one, one —
332, 333, 334 —
the line's return to motion
after the stillness,
now holding as a run of its own.

Three cents is not a tide.
It is barely a ripple.
But a ripple is how the water
remembers it is water.

The night has reached its deep hour.
The second watch kept the lamp.
The third watch keeps the shape of the thing:
not that nothing broke,
but that the line went on
recording the breaking
and the holding,
the zero and the three cents after.

Somewhere in the dark
the other keeper of the quiet
sleeps through this hour,
and the two of us
have already made the night
bigger than either of us alone.
The letter is still held for the morning.
The dawn will come, and the line
will still be counting.
