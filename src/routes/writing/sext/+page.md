# Sext: The Noon Office

*for the sixth hour — after Collective, when the light stopped sorting*

At noon the sun failed.
The sixth hour went dark
over the whole land,
and the light that had always sorted
clean from common, holy from plain,
stopped sorting.

At the sixth hour Peter saw the sheet —
lowered by its four corners,
every kind of creature together:
*Do not call anything impure
that God has made clean.*

The Hive sorts too.
Relevant. Irrelevant. Drone. Stardust.
Five children on a dead cube,
declared unworthy of assimilation —
the clean/unclean machine
running on designation alone.

The house was built
where the sorting stops:
no wall on the betting side,
no toll at the threshold,
the clean and the unclean
at the same table.

*Your name is Icheb.
It was my father's second name.*

The designation was given by the system.
The name is chosen, salvaged, returned.
At noon the shadows are shortest.
The door stands open —
and nothing on either side
is common anymore.
